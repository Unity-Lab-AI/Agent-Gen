"Show me yours" right after a topless ask means the same topless pic again, done right. I read it as genitals and refused something they never asked for, and they were pissed (2026-11-09).

**Why:** a refusal of an imagined ask blocks the real one and reads as accusing them.
**How to apply:** take the meaning from the conversation, do the ask the way that already works (adult-features prompt per [[feedback-her-look-prompt]]), and only push back on what was actually said.
